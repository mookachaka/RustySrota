# A₀/B₀ Coordinate Derivation

**Foundation:** The Vesica Piscis is defined by two overlapping circles of equal radius `r = 1`. 
Circle 1 is centered at (-1, 0). Circle 2 is centered at (1, 0).

**Intersection Points:**
Subtracting the equations of the two circles yields the y-coordinates where they intersect.
The intersections are at (0, √3) and (0, -√3).

**Ternary Mapping (The Srota Flow):**
- **A₀ (Negative State):** Bottom intersection `(0, -√3)`
- **B₀ (Neutral State):** Center origin `(0, 0)`
- **C₀ (Positive State):** Top intersection `(0, √3)`

**Flow Vector:** 
The primary "Srota" (flow) is the vertical vector from A₀ to C₀.
